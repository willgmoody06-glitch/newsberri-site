BEFORE YOU ASK

Things people ask us

Do you tell me what to trade?

No. NewsBerri reads the news and scores what it means for each contract. What you do with that read is yours — we're informational only.

Do you show prices or charts?

Never. No price levels, no candlesticks, anywhere. You already have a platform for that — NewsBerri is the half of the desk it doesn't cover.

What happens after the 7 days?

You're reminded before the trial ends, and you can cancel in one click. If you stay, the rate you joined at is yours for as long as you keep the plan.

Will it run beside my platform?

That's what it's built for. It's a browser terminal on any machine, and Pro adds a desktop overlay that sits on top of your charts.

Which tier should I start on?

Core if you want the whole feed at your desk. Pro if you want it watching while you're away from it. Desk adds a read API for your own tools. All three tiers carry every contract and every source.

Where do the scores come from?

Every headline is scored once, server-side, against each contract it touches — then the same read goes out to everyone at the same moment. Nobody gets a different number to you.
